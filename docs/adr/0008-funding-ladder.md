# 0008. Funding ladder (legal-first)

Date: 2026-10-24
Status: accepted

## Context

Running private worlds, CI, and AI agent tokens costs money. Maintainer labour
is unpaid by default. The project must fund itself without becoming a
pay-to-win pirate shop or redistributing Amazon assets. Publishers that
*allow* community servers (e.g. Facepunch, Bohemia under explicit rules)
typically permit cosmetics / access fees and forbid gameplay power. Amazon has
**not** published an equivalent grant for New World; monetization therefore
carries residual legal risk even when ethically careful.

## Decision

1. Adopt the **F0→F4 ladder** in master plan §0F.
2. **No pay-to-win** and no sale of Amazon content — ever.
3. “Donations” that grant power or rare gear are treated as **sales** and are
   forbidden under our policy (aligns with Bohemia’s donation-vs-sale split).
4. Form a legal entity before F1+ money at scale; keep a public ledger for
   community funds.
5. Prefer **original** cosmetics and merch over anything derived from ripped
   New World assets.
6. Pause F2–F4 immediately if a rights-holder demands it; document the event.

## Consequences

- Landing/store work waits on rename + entity + counsel notes.
- Playable spine remains priority; funding design is parallel scaffolding.
- LEGAL.md carries the human-readable policy.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| Stay forever unpaid | Burns out maintainers; servers die |
| Gold/XP shop | Lawsuit + community poison |
| Ship PAKs for a fee | Clear infringement |
| Wait for Amazon license only | Good if it comes; not a plan to starve until then |
